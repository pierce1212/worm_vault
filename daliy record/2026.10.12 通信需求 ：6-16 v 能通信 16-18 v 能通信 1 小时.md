## 本次记录

**目标** 
<mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">COM_SAFEAVAIL_ 001</mark>: Every network node (ECU) shall allow communication to operate in the range of 6 V to 16 V without time limit and 16 V to 18 V for 1 hour.
Note：When the voltage increases to a level of 0.5 V higher than the specified minimum supply voltage, the communication is resumed.


**实施过程**

| 电压条件         | 通信策略               |     |
| ------------ | ------------------ | --- |
| 6–16 V       | 无时间限制              |     |
| 16 V＜电压≤18 V | 连续高压满一小时后禁止通信      | 🆗  |
| ＞18 V        | 检测到即禁止，不等待一小时      |     |
| ＜6 V         | 禁止，回升到≥6.5 V后才允许恢复 |     |
